    Import your reading history

    Bring in everything you've tracked on Goodreads or StoryGraph: shelves, ratings, dates read and reviews. Covers and descriptions are looked up afterwards.

    How to get the file

    Goodreads: on goodreads.com open My Books, then Import and export (under Tools in the left column) and click Export Library. When it's ready, download the .csv file.

    StoryGraph: open your profile menu, go to Manage Account and choose Export StoryGraph Library, then download the .csv file.
